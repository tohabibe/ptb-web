Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is there a contract?

No. Every PureTalk Business plan is month-to-month. Add lines as you grow and drop them when the season slows, with no termination fees.

What network does PureTalk Business use?

PureTalk Business runs on a nationwide 5G network, the same coverage your crews count on today. 5G requires a compatible device.

How does the 50% off first bill offer work?

All new customers receive 50% off their first bill for wireless service. Devices are not included.

What happens after I use my high-speed data?

After your plan's high-speed allowance, speeds are reduced until your plan renews. You won't be hit with surprise overage charges.

Can I mix different plans on one account?

Yes. Give field crews the $15 Field plan, managers the $30 Pro plan, and tablets or hotspots a $10 Data only line, all on one account.

Who supports my account?

A 100% U.S.-based support team, plus a dedicated business account manager who knows your account and picks up when you call.
